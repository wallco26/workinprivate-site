### Writing Your Year-End Self-Review With AI, Without Pasting Your Company Into a Chatbot
- **Target:** early December 2026 (review season, Nov–Dec)
- **Category:** Guides
- **Angle:** Self-reviews are full of things you shouldn't paste into a cloud chatbot: revenue
  numbers, client names, a coworker's mistakes, salary expectations. Walk through loading notes,
  exported emails and project docs into a knowledge base, having the AI pull out your wins with
  sources, and drafting them in your manager's template. Close with a section on preparing a raise
  conversation.

### Your Year in Money: A Private End-of-Year Financial Clean-Up With AI
- **Target:** mid/late December 2026 (year-end and pre-tax-season)
- **Category:** Guides
- **Angle:** Bank statements, pay stubs and 1099s are the most sensitive files most people own.
  Concrete tasks done offline: find forgotten subscriptions in a statement, sort deductible
  expenses, build a "documents I'll need for taxes" checklist. Not tax advice; it's organizing.
- **Watch out:** Similar format to `open-enrollment-compare-health-plans-privately`. Keep it about
  statements and spending, not insurance.
- **Links to:** `chat-with-your-documents-privately`, `ai-that-knows-less-on-purpose`,
